# Inspirations · 靈感來源

Each sketch started from a kind of sketch that was popular on OpenProcessing in October 2026 (the Trending, Particles and
Math lists of [openprocessing.org/discover](https://openprocessing.org/discover/), and a site search for
harmonographs). None of them reuses code from those sketches: I took the theme and wrote a new piece around it. Where I
could see the original's preview, the difference is described against it; where I could only see the title, that is
said.

每個草稿都從 2026 年 10 月 OpenProcessing 上受歡迎的一類作品出發（探索頁的 Trending、Particles、Math 清單與「harmonograph」
搜尋結果），沒有沿用原作的程式碼，只借用題材並重新創作。能看到原作預覽圖的，差異以預覽為準；只看得到標題的，會另外註明。
